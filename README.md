# Unsent letters

<img width="2000" height="2000" alt="unsent letters" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e47bf7e-4420-462e-bef7-6f952bb089bf" />

Unsent Letter is a platform where people can write letters to someone they have things to say to but can't express in person. The letters are publicly shared, allowing others to read and relate to them, while the intended recipient probably won't see them. It's a space to express your feelings, share memories, and say the things left unsaid.
