to my younger self

Dear younger me, I walked by a park today and I saw a little girl play with her friends. It looked alot like the one we used to go to and have fun. I remembered how we used to play with our friends and how we used to laugh and have fun. I wish I could go back to those days and relive those moments. I hope you are doing well and that you are happy. Remember to always be kind to yourself and others. You are doing great, keep going!
