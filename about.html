About the Project

Sometimes we have words that we want to say, but can't. Thats why I created this platform. A safe-space for people to express their feelings and thoughts without fear of judgement. You can write a letter, read letters from others, or just explore the platform.

I personally have had many times where I wanted to say something to someone, whether it was emotional, harsh or even a rant. I always wished there was somewhere I could take all those feelings out so I decided to create this platform. I hope it helps you helps you get your thoughts off your chest.

This project was created by Musfirah, Enjoy!
